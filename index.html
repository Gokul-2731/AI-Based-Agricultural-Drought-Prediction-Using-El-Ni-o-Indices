The project

Connecting climate patterns to conditions on the ground.

Drought develops over time and can affect crops, soil, and rural livelihoods. This project explores a data-driven way to study those risks.

01

El Niño indices

Large-scale ocean and atmosphere patterns provide useful context for understanding seasonal climate variability.

02

Satellite data

Earth observation data can help describe vegetation and environmental conditions across agricultural regions.

03

Machine learning

Data patterns can be studied together to support research into agricultural drought prediction.
